04 / EXPERIMENT

How the model was selected

Both models learn only from the earliest 60% of transactions. Validation AP chooses the model; the latest 20% remains held out.

AP means average precision, a ranking metric. Brier score measures probabilistic error; neither establishes calibration or real-world performance. The dataset and labels were generated by a known simulation, making these results illustrative only.

READING THE RESULTS

Signals, not verdicts.

Precision answers: “Of the transactions flagged for review, how many have positive labels?”

Recall answers: “Of all positive labels, how many did we flag?”

A larger review queue may capture more positives, but it also consumes more reviewer capacity.
